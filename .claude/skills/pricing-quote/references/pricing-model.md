# Pricing Model — default guide

*Used when the user has not uploaded their own guide. If they have, theirs wins.*

Every quote = **package base × industry adjustment × country multiplier + add-ons**. Round to the client's currency.

## Payment terms — always

- 50% deposit before work starts
- 50% on handover, before domain transfer and final key rotation
- First month of Care due at handover
- Extras quoted in writing before doing them
- Cash markets: deposit in hand

## Rounding

Quote in the client's currency, rounded to a clean figure. Prefer numbers ending in 00 or 50. Never present a converted figure with odd digits.
